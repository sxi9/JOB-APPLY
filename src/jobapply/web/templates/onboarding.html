Advanced: IMAP / SMTP hosts

The test logs in over IMAP, reads only the subject of the newest message, then logs in over SMTP and disconnects. Nothing is sent, marked read or deleted. The password is encrypted at rest and never shown again; you can change or remove it later under Settings → Mailbox.
